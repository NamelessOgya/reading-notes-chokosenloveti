4. **Thurstone, Louis L. (1927)**: *A Law of Comparative Judgement*. Psychological Review, 34(4), 273–286.

8. **Domencich, Thomas A. and McFadden, Daniel (1975)**: *Urban Travel Demand: A Behavioral Analysis*. North-Holland Publishing Company.
